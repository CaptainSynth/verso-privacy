Verso — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Verso shows lyrics for music that is playing in other apps on your phone and on Android Auto.

What Verso reads

  • Now-playing information (title, artist, album, length, playback position and album art) from the media session of the app that is playing. Verso needs *notification access* only to read these media sessions; it does not read, store or send the content of your notifications.
  • The queue and buttons that the music app publishes on its media session, so you can jump to a song, like it or search for a song to play in that app. Searches you speak are passed to that music app only.

What leaves your device

  • To find lyrics, Verso sends the title, artist, album and length of the playing song to lyrics services. Nothing else is sent to them: no device identifiers, no location, no listening history.
  • When you type a search for a song, the text you type is sent to a music catalogue service to suggest matching songs, and is then passed to your music app to play the song you pick. Nothing else is sent with it.
  • Only if you connect a music account (YouTube or Spotify, in Settings): when you like a song or add it to a playlist, Verso sends that song's id (or its title and artist, to find it) and the playlist to that service, using the sign-in you approved on the service's own page. Verso never sees your password, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • Each of these services handles the requests under its own privacy terms.

Purchases

  • Verso Pro is bought through Google Play. Verso never sees payment details; it only asks Google Play whether your account owns Pro, and remembers the answer on the phone.

What is stored on your device

  • Lyrics that were found (a local cache), song covers shown in lists, your recent searches and recently played songs (for search suggestions), your settings, your Verso playlists and lines you saved. If you connect a music account, its sign-in tokens, encrypted with a key kept in Android's Keystore and never backed up. They never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall Verso or clear its data.
  • On-device translation (ML Kit) downloads language models from Google the first time it is used.

What Verso does not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no selling or sharing of data. If Verso closes unexpectedly, a crash report is kept on the phone only; it is sent to the developer only if you choose to share it.

Children

Verso is not directed at children under 13.
