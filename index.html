Verso — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Verso shows lyrics for music that is playing in other apps on your phone and on Android Auto.

What Verso reads

  • Now-playing information (title, artist, album, length, playback position and album art) from the media session of the app that is playing. Verso needs *notification access* only to read these media sessions; it does not read, store or send the content of your notifications.
  • The queue and buttons that the music app publishes on its media session, so you can jump to a song, like it or search for a song to play in that app. Searches you type or speak are passed to that music app only.

What leaves your device

  • To find lyrics, Verso sends the title, artist, album and length of the playing song to the lyrics services you have enabled (LRCLIB, Better Lyrics, YouLyPlus, KuGou). Nothing else is sent to them: no device identifiers, no location, no listening history.
  • Only if you connect a music account (YouTube or Spotify, in Settings): when you like a song or add it to a playlist, Verso sends that song's id (or its title and artist, to find it) and the playlist to that service, using the sign-in you approved on the service's own page. Verso never sees your password, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • Each service has its own privacy terms. You can turn any service off in Sources.

What is stored on your device

  • Lyrics that were found (a local cache), song covers shown in lists, your settings, your Verso playlists and lines you saved. If you connect a music account, its sign-in tokens, encrypted with a key kept in Android's Keystore and never backed up. They never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall Verso or clear its data.
  • On-device translation (ML Kit) downloads language models from Google the first time it is used.

What Verso does not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no selling or sharing of data.

Children

Verso is not directed at children under 13.
